# VPS price comparison for the portfolio deployment

Checked October 1, 2026. Prices are advertised Linux VM charges, excluding model inference, domains, external backups, and applicable taxes unless stated. USD and EUR are kept separate; no exchange-rate assumptions. The intended workload is the Python web app, Neo4j, and small local embeddings, with chat inference elsewhere. An 8 GB VM is a sensible starting estimate, not a measured capacity requirement.

## Assessment

These are self-administered servers. Plan features do not establish latency, CPU consistency, or application capacity; no provider benchmarking was performed. For an experiment, UpCloud Starter is a useful flexible alternative at $20/month for 8 GB, or $24 with seven-day daily backups, subject to measuring storage and memory needs. That is half DigitalOcean's server-only 8 GB price. Paying hourly avoids a long commitment before measuring the app.

For a stable year-long demo, compare RackNerd's roughly $10/month 8 GB annual offer with OVH's $8.50 annual starting offer. Both need a final availability and checkout check. Hostinger offers included backups but requires two years upfront for its advertised intro rate. Contabo looks inexpensive on advertised RAM, with more uncertainty in the verified price conditions. Netcup's current generation is considerably pricier than cached G12 search results and requires care around term, location, and VAT. IONOS's three-month introductory price should not be mistaken for the ongoing cost.

For this project, I would shortlist UpCloud for a short trial and RackNerd or OVH for a longer inexpensive deployment after measuring performance. Hetzner EU remains competitive if stock returns. All choices still require app and database maintenance. No resources were purchased or deployed.
