## Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Make the selected search's terminal outcome clear without moving
the reader away from their reading position.

**Current behavior:**
Polling changes ordinary outcome and metadata text. Readers can miss the change
from searching to completed. A guide update may still show previously saved
reading, so visible articles alone do not establish that the latest search ended.

**Desired behavior:**
When the selected attempt changes from running to terminal, present a prominent
outcome notice and announce it through the reader's accessible status mechanism.
Use the actual attempt outcome, including empty, Declined, needs editing, failed,
cancelled, and interrupted outcomes. Keep partial-search and Deferred disclosures
visible. Successful retrieval must be distinguishable from retained reading after
an unsuccessful update. Repeated observations of the same terminal attempt must
not repeat the transition announcement. Opening an already terminal guide should
show its outcome without pretending a search just finished.

**Key interfaces:**
- `SavedSourceGuide.latest_attempt` and `GuideAttempt.attempt_id`, `status`, and
  `outcome` identify the observed attempt and its terminal result.
- `last_retrieved_at`, guide outcome, and existing disclosures distinguish saved
  content from the latest attempt.
- The Source Guide reader's status polling and accessible outcome presentation
  own the notice. Reuse the persisted lifecycle rather than add another one.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A selected running attempt that finishes with articles presents a visible
      completion notice and an accessible announcement for that attempt.
- [ ] Empty results, Declined, needs editing, failures, cancellation, and
      interruption show their actual outcomes rather than success wording.
- [ ] Partial-search and Deferred disclosures remain available after completion.
- [ ] Repeated refreshes of the same terminal attempt do not repeat the
      transition announcement. A later explicit update can announce its own end.
- [ ] Opening or reloading a terminal guide shows its saved outcome without a
      false new-completion announcement.
- [ ] An unsuccessful update identifies its own outcome and preserves previous
      reading and its successful retrieval timestamp.
- [ ] Completion does not scroll, move keyboard focus, switch the selected
      guide, or pull a reader back from New question or Edit question.
- [ ] A background guide completing does not announce completion as belonging
      to the selected guide. Its persisted outcome remains available on opening.

**Out of scope:**
- Desktop notifications, sounds, scheduled monitoring, or new backend states.
- Layout prototypes, question submission transitions, and article grouping.
- Retrieval, prompt, persistence, or model-quality changes.

## Validation

Exercise reader behavior with controlled running and terminal guide responses,
including repeated observations, changed attempt identities, retained content,
and selection changes. Assert reader-visible outcomes and accessible behavior,
not DOM snapshots or private rendering state. Reuse the existing deterministic
application tests for lifecycle and retention, and run relevant checks with uv.
