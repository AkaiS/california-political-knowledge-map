# A topic edge hangs off the Source Article, not the Article Chunk

`ontology.md` declares `DISCUSSES_TOPIC` from `:NewsArticle` **or** `:Chunk` to `:PolicyArea` — a direct thematic edge, permitted at either level. The News Ontology admits only the `:NewsArticle` half. The `:Chunk` half is retired for news, and nothing in this pipeline writes it.

A Policy Area is decided once per Source Article, over its coreference-resolved text, by Policy Area Classification. Nothing per-chunk decides it, so writing the article's decision onto every chunk would be fanning one judgement out over text that never earned it: an article about housing carries paragraphs about a committee chair's biography and a neighbour's commute, and a chunk-level edge asserts each of those is about housing exactly as strongly as the paragraph the classification was actually reading. The edge would then be false in the one place it gets used. A topic edge names no Article Chunk for the same reason: what grounds it is the article, and the chunks reached through the article are the Grounding Evidence behind it.

Narrowing an endpoint is not a correction to the primary ontology and does not engage the bar in [ADR 0014](0014-the-primary-ontology-is-authored-not-frozen.md). `ontology.md` still declares both halves, and it is right to: a chunk-level topic edge is a coherent structure, and a source that classified at a finer grain than an article could supply one. What is retired is news's ability to produce it. That is precisely the subsetting the News Ontology exists to do — but this subsetting went unrecorded, which is what this decision fixes.

It went unrecorded because nothing in the ontology package can hold it. A `Divergence` is property-scoped: it names one property and the labels or relationships it applies to, in either direction, and there is no property here — the property set of `DISCUSSES_TOPIC` is unchanged, and it is an endpoint that left. A Shortcut Rule is the other declaration available, and it records a path news flattens through an intermediate node; no path was flattened, and there is no skipped node to name. The conformance check accepts the result silently, because a news relationship whose endpoints are a subset of the primary shape passes on the subset test alone. So a permitted endpoint can disappear from the news half of the model and every mechanism built to catch drift will agree that nothing happened. An ADR is the only place the reason can live.

## Considered options

Writing the edge at both levels keeps the primary ontology's permission intact and costs nothing at write time, since the classification is already in hand. It is rejected on what it asserts, not on cost: it turns one article-level judgement into N chunk-level claims that no stage made, and retrieval cannot tell them apart from a claim some future per-chunk classifier did make.

Classifying per chunk to earn the chunk-level edge honestly is the version of that with the assertion repaired, and it is a different pipeline, not a different write. It buys finer-grained retrieval at the price of one model call per chunk and of a per-chunk answer that news prose frequently cannot support — a chunk is a contiguous block of text, not a self-contained argument, and the article's subject is often stated once, in the lede. It stays available: the primary ontology still permits the edge, and this decision retires a write, not a possibility.

Recording the narrowing as a `Divergence` is the closest fit the ontology package offers, and it still does not fit. Stretching `Divergence` to carry an endpoint would give the type two subjects and one field name, and the divergence record — which reads as "this property, in this direction, for these types" — would have to be read differently depending on which kind of gap it held. A `Divergence` also says a gap is unresolved and waiting on an authoritative source. This gap is closed and deliberate; nothing is pending.

## Consequences

`news.py` declares `DISCUSSES_TOPIC` with `NewsArticle` as its only source label, and the code comment there states the problem the shape solves and points here for the decision. `PolicyArea` sits in `STAGE_DECIDED_LABELS` rather than in the extracted labels, so a `PolicyArea` node or a topic relationship arriving from the chunk extractor is dropped by conformance and counted in the Adherence Report, and the write path refuses one outright — the narrowing holds at both the conformance seam and the graph write, not by convention.

`news-ontology.md` is generated from `news.py`, so its relationship table shows one source for `DISCUSSES_TOPIC` and its divergence record shows nothing beside it. That is the intended state and not an omission to repair: the file records what the news pipeline can produce, and the narrowing survives every regeneration for free, while the reason is here. Someone reading only the two tables sees the primary ontology permit two sources and the news ontology permit one, with no explanation in either document — this ADR is the link, and adding it to the generator would put an argument in a file that is meant to be regenerated without one.

Any further endpoint narrowing takes the same route. The conformance check will not catch it, so an unwritten narrowing is invisible until someone diffs two documents and asks; this is the second place, after `Divergence`'s own scope, where the ontology package's silence has to be made up for in prose.
