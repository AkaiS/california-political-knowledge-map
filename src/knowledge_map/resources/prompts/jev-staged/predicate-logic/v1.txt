Decide how the requested relationships combine. Choose conjunction when the question independently requires multiple predicates, such as organizations lobbying for one Bill AND against another Bill, or both endorsing and funding a campaign. Choose alternatives when a single requested relationship can have any of the listed outcomes, such as lobbying for OR against a Bill, or retrieving a vote regardless of its outcome. A predicate union represents alternatives and cannot express a conjunction. Choose unclear if the question does not establish the combination.
